# Research — Thailand market & the 2026 registration law

## The regulatory moment (why now)
- Bangkok's *Ordinance on the Control of Animal Keeping or Abandonment* takes effect **10 Jan 2026**:
  every dog & cat must be **registered + microchipped** (within 120 days of birth / 30 days of adoption),
  with fines up to **฿25,000**. Free microchip drives run across BMA's 50 districts.
- Stated goals: a database to **monitor pets, reunite lost animals, and reduce strays**.

## The gap Biometric-Pet-Identification fills
- A microchip is a **scan-only RFID transponder** — needs a proprietary scanner (vets/shelters only),
  is **not a tracker**, and stores only a 15-digit code. A member of the public who finds a stray cannot
  use it. Owners have publicly wished chips "had GPS."
- A **smartphone biometric** (nose-print + face) needs no hardware → anyone can identify a found stray.

## Market
- Global pet-microchip market ≈ USD 0.45B (2024) → ~0.63B (2033), CAGR ~3.8%; **Asia-Pacific flagged as a
  key low-penetration growth region**. Rising Thai pet ownership; emerging registries (Thai National Pet Registry).
- Adjacent demand: **pet insurers** want identity verification to reduce false claims (a cited driver in the
  nose-print literature).
